IDENTIFICATION

Who we are

Woundwort builds the interfaces people use when the stakes are real.

We design and ship interaction systems for teams that can’t afford vague flows, decorative components, or AI that makes up its own visual language.

OPERATING AREA

Between the research and the code

Most of our work happens in the gap between what the research found and what ships. That means component specs precise enough to build from, and state coverage that matches the edge cases your users actually hit.

It also means Claude tools that stay locked to your tokens, your spacing rules and your accessibility requirements, instead of sliding back into generic UI.

DOCTRINE

Approach

We treat every interface as a set of decisions made under constraint. Research, technical limits, brand rules and user context aren’t soft inputs. They are the brief.

What we hand over is the smallest set of interfaces that solves the problem, with nothing decorative bolted on.

Woundwort is the working name for the practice. The work is the product.
